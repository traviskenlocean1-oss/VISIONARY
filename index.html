Why Not WordPress?

WordPress sites are slower, harder to secure, and require constant updates. A hand-coded site loads in under 1 second, never gets hacked through plugin vulnerabilities, and doesn't need monthly maintenance just to stay online.

Why Does Speed Matter?

Google uses page speed as a ranking factor. A site that loads in 0.8 seconds ranks higher and converts better than one that takes 4 seconds. Every second of load time costs you customers.

What Does "You Own It" Mean?

Your files, your domain, your hosting. Everything is transferred to you when we're done. You're never locked into paying us just to keep your site running.
