A Note On Tools

Why We Don't Use WordPress, Wix, or Squarespace

WordPress Is Slow

The average WordPress site takes 3–5 seconds to load. Page speed is a Google ranking factor — every extra second costs you rankings and customers. Our hand-coded sites load in under 1 second.

WordPress Gets Hacked

Over 90% of hacked websites are on WordPress. Outdated plugins and themes are constant vulnerabilities. A hand-coded static site has zero plugins — nothing to exploit.

Templates Look Generic

When you use a Squarespace or Wix template, so do thousands of other businesses. Your competitors might be running the exact same design. Custom code means your site is unique — period.

You Pay Forever

Wix, Squarespace, and most WordPress hosts charge monthly forever. Stop paying, your site goes down. We build sites you own — no ongoing platform fees to exist online.
