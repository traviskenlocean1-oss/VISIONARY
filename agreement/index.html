2. Scope of Work

The Studio will design and build a custom website for the Client consistent with the selected package below. Work outside this scope is not included and is governed by Section 6 (Change Orders).

  • Starter ($500–750): single-page custom website, mobile-responsive design, contact form, basic on-page SEO, 2 revision rounds.
  • Standard ($750–1,000): up to 5 pages, everything in Starter, custom animations, Google Analytics setup, social media integration, 3 revision rounds.
  • Full Build ($1,000–2,000): up to 8–10 pages, everything in Standard, advanced custom functionality built around the Client's business (which may include e-commerce, booking, membership/gated content, custom integrations or other features as scoped in writing), unlimited revisions within the delivery window in Section 5.
  • Every package includes: setup or cleanup of the Client's Google Business Profile (Google Maps) and Bing Places listing, linked to the new website, and a simple way for customers to leave reviews.

3. Payment Terms

A 50% deposit of the Total Project Price is due before any work begins. The deposit is non-refundable once work has started, as it pays for time and resources already committed.

The remaining 50% is due when the Client approves the completed site, before final files, source code or domain/hosting credentials are transferred or the site is published live.

All payments are one-time, flat fees for the scope in Section 2. No hourly billing applies unless agreed in writing for change orders.

If the final balance is not paid within 7 calendar days of the Studio's completion notice, a late fee of 5% of the outstanding balance applies for each additional 7-day period it remains unpaid, and the Studio may suspend access to the site or take it offline until payment is received in full.

Payment methods and invoice details are provided separately by the Studio.

4. Ownership & Delivery

All designs, code and project files remain the property of the Studio until the Total Project Price is paid in full. Upon full payment, ownership of the completed website transfers to the Client. The Studio may show the completed project in its portfolio and marketing unless the Client asks otherwise in writing.

5. Timeline & Revisions

The estimated delivery above runs from the date the Studio receives all required Client materials (text, images, logo and other content). Delays in Client content or feedback extend the timeline and are not the Studio's responsibility. Revision rounds are limited to the number in the selected package; additional requests are billed as a change order under Section 6.

6. Change Orders

Any request that adds pages, features or functionality beyond the scope in Section 2 is a change order. Change orders require written agreement on the added scope and fee before work on them begins.

7. Cancellation

The Client may cancel in writing at any time. If cancellation happens after the deposit is paid, the deposit is kept by the Studio. If work beyond the deposit stage has been completed, the Client owes a pro-rated fee for that work before any partial deliverables are released.

8. Optional Monthly Maintenance

An optional maintenance plan is available at $100/month, covering content updates, security monitoring, performance optimization and priority support. It is billed separately, may be added any time after launch, and either party may cancel with 14 days' written notice. It is not included in the project price unless confirmed in writing.

9. Limitation of Liability

The Studio's total liability under this Agreement for any claim shall not exceed the amount actually paid by the Client. The Studio is not liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages, lost profits or business interruption, or for outages, errors or policy changes of third-party services (including hosting providers, domain registrars, payment processors and analytics platforms). The Client is responsible for the accuracy and legality of content and materials it supplies.

10. Independent Contractor

The Studio is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

11. Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential any non-public business, technical or financial information shared by the other in connection with this project, except as required by law.

12. Governing Law & Full Agreement

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida. It is the entire agreement between the parties for this project and replaces any earlier discussions or proposals. Any change must be in writing and agreed by both parties.
