Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

What's included in every plan?

Every project includes custom design, mobile responsiveness, contact form setup, basic SEO, and Google Analytics. You own all files when done.

Do I need hosting separately?

Yes — but we set it up for you. Most clients pay $5–$15/month for hosting. We point you to the right provider and handle the configuration.

How long does delivery take?

Starter and Standard plans: 48–72 hours after you send us your content. Full Build: 5–7 days. Redesigns follow the same timeline.

What if I want changes after launch?

That's what the Maintenance plan is for — $100/month for unlimited updates and same-day turnaround. Or pay per change at $50/hour.

Do I own the site when it's done?

100%. Your domain, your hosting account, your code. Everything is transferred into your name. You're never locked into paying us to stay online.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, logo (if you have one), photos, services list, and any copy you'd like to include. We can help write copy if you need it.
