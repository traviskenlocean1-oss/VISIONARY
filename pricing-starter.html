Everything in your Starter site, explained.

01

Fully custom design

I design your site from a blank canvas, not a template. Your layout, typography, color system, and spacing are built around your business and the exact people you're trying to reach. Nothing on your site is copied from a theme ten other businesses already use, so it looks like yours and only yours, which is what builds trust the moment someone lands on it.

02

Mobile-first build

Most of your customers will find you on their phone, so I build and test every page at phone size first, then scale up to desktop. Buttons are easy to tap, text reads clearly without zooming, and nothing spills off the screen. I check the finished site across real device sizes before it ever reaches you.

03

Real on-page SEO

Every page gets a unique title, a meta description written around what you actually do, a clean heading structure, descriptive image alt text, and readable URLs. I also add the structured data that tells search engines your business name, location, and services in a format they can read. That's the foundation that makes you findable from day one.

04

A contact form that actually works

Visitors reach you in seconds through a form that delivers straight to your inbox, with spam protection built in so real messages get through. You never lose a lead to a broken button or a form that goes nowhere. I test the entire form from submission to delivery before launch, so you know it works before you depend on it.

05

Subtle scroll motion

Sections ease into view as visitors scroll, so the page feels alive and deliberate instead of static. The motion is tuned to stay smooth on every device and never slow the page down, and it respects visitors who have reduced motion turned on in their device settings.

You get two full rounds of revisions to get the copy, photos, and details exactly right, and the site is ready in about five business days from the day we start. You see the design before it goes live and have a real say in every page, so there are no surprises at launch.
